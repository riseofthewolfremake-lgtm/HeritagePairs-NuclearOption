Turn this layout guide into a photoreal flat straight-on game texture of the main instrument panel of a WWII North American B-25 Mitchell bomber, restored, with three small modern retrofit avionics units. KEEP THE LAYOUT EXACTLY, pixel for pixel: same canvas, the same ARCHED panel outline (curved top edge, everything outside the outline stays pure white). ALL SIXTEEN BLACK circles (six on the left, ten on the right) stay EMPTY PURE BLACK HOLES of exactly the same size and position, with only a thin dark metal bezel ring: do not paint any instrument into a black circle, real instruments are fitted there later. Only the LIGHT GREY circles become 1940s round aircraft instruments with black faces, white numerals and needles (clock, suction, ammeter, carburettor air temperature, free air temperature, oxygen, compass, de-icer pressure). Every black RECTANGLE stays an empty pure black opening with a thin dark frame. The small orange and red dots become small round red and amber warning-light jewels. The light marks along the bottom become a row of 1940s black bat-handle toggle switches with tiny white stencilled labels. Panel: worn black crinkle paint, small white stencilled labels under the instruments, screws, a few small data placards, faint scuffs. No extra screens, no extra instruments, no perspective, even flat lighting.
